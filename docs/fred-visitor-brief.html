Fred brief · aimez visitor immersion

Design brief for Spatialbound Fred

Use this page as the full brief. Goal: in Spatialbound Design Studio / Fred, design a visitor-facing immersion for aimez.ai visitors, quality-bar comparable to Spatialbound’s own immersive landing, but on aimez identity.

Who aimez is

aimez builds evidence models for decisions about complex physical systems. Sparse observations, constraints, and audit records become inspectable representations before they guide a route, forecast, or research claim. This is not a weather app, not a heat dashboard as product identity, and not an “AI agents do engineering” pitch.

Primary substrate (Manhattan)

Klosr is a city-scale evidence model for pedestrian conditions on a Manhattan walk graph. Thermal comfort / shade / crowding are layers inside that representation. Field-JEPA forecasts the next crowding field from measured camera cycles.

What to design in Spatialbound

  1. Place the scene on Manhattan (roughly Village → Midtown walkable fabric; WSQ / Gansevoort / Midtown corridor ok).
  2. Visitor-first composition: one immersive first view — brand aimez is the hero signal; one short headline; one supporting sentence; one CTA group; dominant spatial visual. No card grid in the hero.
  3. Visual language: dark immersion, cool teal accent (~#5ec8c0), serif brand / clean sans UI. Avoid purple-gradient SaaS look and warm cream + terracotta brochure look.
  4. Show an evidence field over the city (crowding / walk-graph feel), not a weather radar identity.
  5. Optional: pedestrian / crowd simulation that reads as measured-cycle evidence, labeled as research / evidence layer.
  6. Export or present a photoreal / twin flythrough suitable for aimez visitor landing inspiration.

Copy locks (use or paraphrase tightly)

Brand: aimez
Headline: Evidence models for decisions about the physical world
Lede: Sparse observations become inspectable city-scale representations before they guide a route, forecast, or research claim.
CTA: Enter the city evidence field

Hard bans

  • Do not brand this as a weather app, heat map product, or climate dashboard.
  • Do not open with “AI agents are ready to do real engineering.”
  • Do not Midtown-launder or invent demo URLs / product clears.
  • Do not claim DEMO or /beta access.

Success

A Spatialbound scene a visitor would want to enter: city evidence immersion first, aimez brand unmistakable, evidence-gap story clear in one breath.
