aimez

An applied AI research lab in New York called aimez works as the research division of Fifth Avenue Studios LLC and studies how people move through busy streets.

Small businesses that depend on foot traffic can try a closed beta product called klosr to reach people walking nearby.

Every thirty minutes researchers at aimez estimate sidewalk crowding across Manhattan from public street cameras and feed those estimates into the klosr prototype.

Storefront owners in the closed beta can buy physical placements on the blocks where aimez uses the same crowding estimates to find walkers most likely to pass their door.

A team of aimez researchers published a study of killer whale encounters in the Salish Sea under a project called orcast.

Engineers at aimez also build a developer toolkit called wavves so they can resume long-horizon multi-agent workflows from persistent state on disk.

Testers in the klosr prototype can open a walking map that costs nothing and compare shaded routes with quieter routes before heading out.
